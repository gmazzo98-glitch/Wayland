# I14 Results: People and contact finder (who to call and the published ways to reach them)

*Completed 2026-10-27 by agent antigravity-8c2f*

## What I built

- `intel/people.py`: Shared taxonomy, classification, and contact parsing engine for executives and business contact channels.
  - Role classification (`classify_role`): categorizes titles across 6 functional groups (`general_management`, `operations_technology`, `commercial_export`, `procurement_supply_chain`, `board_parent`, `signatory`, `other`). Maps Italian and international job titles to standard English labels.
  - Pilot sponsor priority ranking (`rank_pilot_sponsors`): ranks potential pilot champions into 6 tiers (Tier 1 General Management down to Tier 6 Other/Advisory), ordering operational leaders above foreign/holding board members.
  - Business contact point extractors (`extract_phones`, `extract_emails`, `extract_pec`, `detect_languages`): parses switchboard phone numbers (E.164 / Italian landlines), department/generic mailboxes, verified Italian certified email (PEC), and detects multi-lingual site support.
  - Strict privacy & suppression engine (`is_suppressed`, `is_generic_business_email`): enforces compliance with decision G3, suppressing non-business mailboxes (`abuse@`, `postmaster@`, `privacy@`, `dpo@`, `noreply@`), personal email guessing, and database placeholder texts.
  - Clean name parsing (`clean_name`): strips Italian honorifics (`Dott.`, `Ing.`, `Sig.`), conjunctions, and multiline noise.
- `intel/collectors/contacts.py`: Collector exposing `COLLECTOR = Collector(name="contacts", ticket="I14")`.
  - Discovers contact pages across common Italian and English paths (`/contatti`, `/contact`, `/chi-siamo`, `/contacts`).
  - Emits ledger facts for `contact.switchboard`, `contact.generic_email`, `contact.pec`, `contact.contact_page`, and `contact.languages`.
  - Grounded with verbatim quote matching via `quote_in_text`.
- `intel/collectors/people.py`: Collector exposing `COLLECTOR = Collector(name="people", ticket="I14")`.
  - Harvesters:
    1. Registry roster (`company_people`): reads verified official filings (Grade A) for legal representatives, directors, signatories (`procuratori`), and statutory auditors.
    2. Own-site leadership pages: extracts operating management from company team and governance pages (Grade B).
    3. Press releases, deals, and directory profiles: identifies operating executives (General Managers, Export Managers, Business Development Directors) from M&A announcements and financial insider profiles (Grade C).
  - Emits specific ledger facts: `people.general_manager`, `people.managing_director`, `people.export_manager`, `people.business_development`, `people.parent_executive`, `people.signatory`, `people.board_member`, and summary `people.pilot_sponsors`.
  - Robust entity boundary matching (`_entity_matches`): ensures press articles and directory profiles match the target company with word boundaries, eliminating false positive leakages across companies.
- `tests/test_intel_people.py`: Comprehensive offline unit test suite (14 test cases) covering:
  - Role classification and parent company director separation.
  - Pilot sponsor tier ranking.
  - Contact extraction (phones, emails, PEC, languages).
  - Privacy suppression filters and generic email detection.
  - End-to-end collector execution with recorded pages and zero network dependencies.

## Decisions taken and why

1. **Decision G2 (LinkedIn Policy)**: User explicitly approved adhering to public company pages and search snippets only (`LINKEDIN_CRAWLER_ENABLED=false`). No login credentials, session cookies, or authenticated crawling are used.
2. **Decision G3 (Contact Point Policy)**: User explicitly approved collecting only published business contact points with exact URL and verbatim quote. Guessed personal email patterns (`name.surname@...`) are strictly prohibited and never emitted. Generic department mailboxes (`info@`, `sales@`, `export@`) and official PEC addresses are collected.
3. **Pilot Sponsor Prioritization Hierarchy**: In industrial Mittelstand companies acquired by multinational groups (such as ASCO Filtri by Mott / IDEX), registry board members are frequently non-resident corporate officers (e.g. Steven Samp, US). The collector ranks operational site managers (GM, MD, Export Manager, Plant Director) at Tiers 1-3 above non-operating parent board members (Tier 5), providing actionable targeting for startup pilot brokering.
4. **Entity Isolation in Press Discovery**: Applied strict regex word boundary matching for legal names and brands to prevent false positives when crawling multi-company deal announcements.

## What the next agent must know

- `intel/people.py` is the central module for all personnel and contact logic: use its functions (`classify_role`, `rank_pilot_sponsors`, `is_suppressed`, `clean_name`) across other collectors if person or contact metadata is needed.
- `intel/collectors/contacts.py` and `intel/collectors/people.py` run independently and can be included via `--collector contacts --collector people` in `scripts/intel_run.py`.
- Both collectors are pure: they receive `CollectorContext`, return `CollectorResult`, and never write directly to the database or mutate `company_people`.
