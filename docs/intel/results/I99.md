## What I built
* `intel/collectors/deep_site.py`: removed the two hard-coded spots the golden-leak guard flagged.
  * **Registered office:** an enumeration of Italian cities (it contained five towns of the golden companies: Binasco, Lainate, Cassano d'Adda, Sernaglia, Laives) became a generic Italian address *shape*: street, 5-digit postcode, capitalised locality, optional province.
  * **Timeline:** a pattern naming ASCO's owner, deal and foreign subsidiaries (`abu dhabi|pune|ascorp|mott deal|red-fish ...`) became (a) dated sentences with a generic corporate-event vocabulary (`_EVENT_WORDS`) and (b) a structural parser for the usual HTML history timeline (the year alone on a line, its entry on the next lines). Value cap raised from 350 to 1,600 characters so later milestones are not cut off.
* `tests/test_intel_deep_site_generic.py`: regression tests on invented companies and towns that appear in no golden file.
* `tests/test_intel_board.py` (separate commit): proposed tickets need a `--why`, finished tickets need any results note under `docs/intel/results/`.
